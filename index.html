Operating rules

Completion week: 3 Full + 2 Duty sessions. Up to 2 Minimum sessions may count.

Progression: top of rep range at ≤RPE 8 → progress. RPE 8–8.5 → hold. RPE ≥9 early → hold/reduce.

Yellow: reduce sets ~25%, shorten conditioning ~25%, remove optional work.

Red: recovery, Minimum, or skip and resume later.

Workdays: protect sleep. Post-shift sessions stay short.

Core rule: Never sacrifice tomorrow's readiness just to win today's workout.
